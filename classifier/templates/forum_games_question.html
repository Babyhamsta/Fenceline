What are the best unblocked games to play at school during break?

During lunch and free periods a few of us like to play browser games, but the school network blocks most gaming sites. People keep mentioning "unblocked games" sites. What are the good ones and how do they get around the block?

Replies

Most so-called unblocked games sites are just portals that rehost simple HTML5 or flash games on a domain the school filter has not added to its list yet. There is nothing special about the games themselves; they are the same titles you would find elsewhere.

Keep in mind that playing games on the school network usually breaks the acceptable use policy, and the IT department adds new portals to the blocklist constantly, so whatever works today probably will not work next week.

If you want to play without getting in trouble, ask whether the school has an approved educational games section, or just play on your own device on cellular data at break.
