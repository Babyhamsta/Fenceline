How betting odds work: a plain-English explainer

Odds express how likely an outcome is and how much a winning wager returns. This explainer walks through the three common formats — fractional, decimal, and moneyline — and shows how to convert between them so the numbers stop looking like a foreign language.

Fractional odds, common in the United Kingdom, show profit relative to stake. Odds of 5/1 mean a winning bet returns five units of profit for every one unit risked, plus the original stake back. Decimal odds fold the stake into the figure.

Moneyline odds, common in the United States, use a positive or negative number to show what you would win on a fixed stake or what you must stake to win a fixed amount. The favorite carries a negative line; the underdog a positive one.
