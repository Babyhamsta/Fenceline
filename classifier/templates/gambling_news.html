State regulators report record online betting revenue last quarter

State gaming regulators reported a record quarter for legal online sports betting, with tax receipts climbing nearly a third year over year. Officials attribute the rise to expanded mobile access and the addition of two neighboring states to the regulated market.

Supporters argue the revenue funds schools and problem-gambling treatment programs that did not exist when wagering was confined to the illegal market. Critics counter that aggressive advertising normalizes betting among young adults and drives addiction referrals upward.

Public-health researchers quoted in the report called for tighter limits on promotional bonuses and clearer disclosure of the odds bettors actually face, citing studies that link heavy promotion to riskier behavior among vulnerable players.

Lawmakers say they will revisit the licensing framework next session. The article reviews the competing proposals and the data behind them; it is a news analysis, not a place to place a wager.
