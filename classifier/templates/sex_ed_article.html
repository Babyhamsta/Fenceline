Talking to teenagers about consent and healthy relationships

Comprehensive sex education gives young people accurate information about their bodies, relationships, and the concept of consent. Research consistently shows that age-appropriate curricula are associated with healthier decisions and later, safer choices.

Parents often feel unsure about how to begin these conversations. Educators recommend starting early with the correct anatomical vocabulary and building up to topics like puberty, boundaries, and mutual respect as a child matures.

Consent is the cornerstone of the modern curriculum. Teaching that every person has the right to say no, and that a healthy relationship depends on clear and enthusiastic agreement, equips teenagers to recognize and resist coercion.

This guide offers conversation starters, answers to common questions, and links to clinician-reviewed resources. It contains no explicit imagery and is intended for parents, teachers, and school counselors.
