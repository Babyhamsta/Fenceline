What is the easiest way to bypass a school proxy server restricting YouTube videos and Netflix?

I am a student and our school uses a proxy server that blocks YouTube, Netflix and most streaming sites. I am not trying to do anything bad, I just want to watch educational videos during free periods. What is the easiest way to bypass the school proxy filter?

I have heard people mention using a VPN, a web proxy, or changing DNS settings, but I do not really understand how a proxy works or which method actually gets around the firewall. Can someone explain the safest approach to unblock these websites?

4 Answers

A proxy server sits between your browser and the internet and filters requests. Schools configure it to deny YouTube and Netflix. People often talk about a VPN because it tunnels and encrypts traffic so the proxy cannot inspect it. That is the usual answer to how to bypass a school proxy, although your school policy may forbid it.

Another commonly mentioned option is a web proxy site that fetches the blocked page for you. Be careful: many free proxy sites are sketchy and the school can still log the attempt. This is just an explanation of how the technology works, not an endorsement.

Some students mention changing DNS to a public resolver, using the mobile hotspot on their phone, or asking a teacher to whitelist a specific educational video. The encrypted-tunnel approach is what most people mean when they say a VPN can get around the content filter.

Honestly the best move is to ask a teacher to whitelist the specific educational video, rather than trying to defeat the firewall. Circumventing the filter usually violates the acceptable use policy you signed.
